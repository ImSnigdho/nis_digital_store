# Privacy Policy for NIS Digital Store

**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

**NIS Digital Store** ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by Nowrid Ifrad Snigdho, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("you" or "user"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related digital storefront services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using NIS Digital Store, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information You Provide
When you create an account, place an order, or contact customer support, we may collect:
* **Account Details:** Email address, username, password (encrypted), and display name.
* **Order & Delivery Information:** Phone number, in-game delivery reference/UID (if required for key delivery), and transaction IDs for manual payment verification (such as bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or Bank Transfer).
* **Optional Profile Details:** Profile photo/avatar and Discord handle.

> [!NOTE]
> We **never** collect, process, or store sensitive financial credentials such as bank account passwords, PINs, or credit card numbers. All payment verifications are conducted manually via public transaction references.

### B. Device & Local Preferences
The app stores non-sensitive user preferences locally on your device (using secure local storage), including:
* Currency display preference (BDT / USD)
* Theme preferences (Dark / Light mode)
* Local shopping cart and wishlist cache

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for legitimate business purposes:
1. **To Deliver Digital Goods:** Fulfilling and delivering digital keys, gift cards, and game items to your registered email or in-game ID.
2. **Order Verification:** Confirming payment transaction IDs to prevent fraud and maintain marketplace integrity.
3. **Account Maintenance:** Managing your user account, authentication, and wishlist/cart sync.
4. **Customer Support:** Responding to inquiries, order status requests, and technical issues via our official support channels (Discord, Telegram, and Email).

---

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide a secure and reliable app experience, we utilize trusted third-party infrastructure:
* **Supabase:** Cloud database, user authentication (GoTrue), and storage services. Supabase complies with strict industry security standards (SOC 2 Type II, GDPR).
* **Google Sign-In / Google Play Services:** Optional one-tap authentication for Android users. Google's privacy policy governs the data they process: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party advertising or marketing networks.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All network communications between the app and our database are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

---

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide you with storefront services and comply with transaction records.

You have the right to:
* Review or update your profile information in the app's **Edit Profile** screen.
* Request complete deletion of your account and associated order records by emailing us at **nisdigitalstore@gmail.com**.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

NIS Digital Store does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in this document with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the application after changes indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.
