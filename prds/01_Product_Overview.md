# Product Overview

**Problem statement:** A mobile shopping app and a website designed for users to easily browse, search, filter, and purchase products securely.

**Solution summary:** Our app and website provide a seamless shopping experience with features like secure checkout, order tracking, and push notifications.

**Target audience:** Casual and frequent online shoppers, particularly those located in Bangladesh.

**Core value proposition:** Our app and website offer an accessible, user-friendly, and secure shopping platform with a simple and intuitive interface.

**Measurable success goals:**

1. Increase user engagement by 20% within the first three months of launch.
2. Achieve a 15% conversion rate for new users within the first month.
3. Reduce cart abandonment rate by 10% within the first six months of launch.
